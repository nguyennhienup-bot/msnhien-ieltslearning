✍️ Essay Builder

Build your essay step-by-step with guidance

Step 1: Write Your Introduction

Your introduction should:

  • Paraphrase the given topic
  • Clearly state your opinion (agree/disagree)
  • Preview your main ideas (optional)
Word count: 0 words
Example Introduction:

"The statement that modern technology has negatively affected human relationships is a contentious issue. I strongly disagree with this view, as technology has actually enhanced communication and brought people closer together."

Step 2: Write Body Paragraph 1 (First Reason)

Include:

  • Topic sentence stating your first argument
  • Explanation or evidence
  • Specific example
Word count: 0 words
Step 3: Write Body Paragraph 2 (Second Reason)

Make sure this argument is different from paragraph 1.

Word count: 0 words
Step 4: Write Body Paragraph 3 (Optional - Third Reason)

If you feel you need a third argument, add it here.

Word count: 0 words
Step 5: Write Your Conclusion

Your conclusion should:

  • Restate your opinion
  • Summarize your main arguments briefly
  • End with a strong closing statement
Word count: 0 words
Example Conclusion:

"In conclusion, I firmly believe that technology has strengthened human relationships rather than weakening them. Technology enables long-distance communication, helps people find communities, and facilitates collaboration. Therefore, society should embrace technology as a tool for human connection."
