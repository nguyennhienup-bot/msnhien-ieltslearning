Exercise 1: Vocational Training vs University

While many people go to university for academic study, more people should be encouraged to do vocational training because there is a lack of qualified workers such as electricians and plumbers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

💡 Tips & Ideas:

✓ Think about job market demands
✓ Consider skills development in both paths
✓ Think about economic benefits
✓ Consider career prospects

📌 Sample Ideas if you AGREE:

Critical shortage of skilled tradespeople in most countries
Vocational training provides faster job entry
Higher earning potential in skilled trades

📌 Sample Ideas if you DISAGREE:

University education provides broader knowledge
Professional careers require academic qualification
Personal choice should be respected

Exercise 2: Technology and Reading Skills

The increasing use of computers and mobile phones has had a negative effect on young people's reading and writing skills. Do you agree or disagree?

💡 Tips & Ideas:

✓ Think about how technology changes communication
✓ Consider different types of reading and writing
✓ Reflect on educational technology benefits
✓ Consider generational differences

📌 Sample Ideas if you AGREE:

Digital communication reduces formal writing practice
Text speak and abbreviations affect grammar
Screen time replaces reading time

📌 Sample Ideas if you DISAGREE:

More reading content available online than ever
New forms of digital literacy are emerging
Educational apps and tools improve skills

Exercise 3: Global Help and Local Communities

We cannot help everyone in the world that needs help, so we should only be concerned with our own communities and countries. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

💡 Tips & Ideas:

✓ Consider moral and ethical responsibilities
✓ Think about resource limitations
✓ Reflect on global interdependence
✓ Balance local and global perspectives

📌 Sample Ideas if you AGREE:

Limited resources require priority setting
Local problems deserve immediate attention
Government responsibility to citizens first

📌 Sample Ideas if you DISAGREE:

Humanitarian obligation to help globally
Global issues affect everyone eventually
Interconnected world requires cooperation

Exercise 4: Unpaid Community Work for Teenagers

Some people think that all teenagers should be required to do unpaid work in their free time to help the local community. They believe this would benefit both the individual teenager and society as a whole. Do you agree or disagree?

💡 Tips & Ideas:

✓ Consider personal development and learning
✓ Think about free time and teenage life
✓ Reflect on community benefit
✓ Consider mandatory vs voluntary participation

📌 Sample Ideas if you AGREE:

Develops sense of responsibility and civic duty
Builds work ethic and social awareness
Creates community engagement

📌 Sample Ideas if you DISAGREE:

Unfair to mandate unpaid labor
Teenagers need free time for studies
Should be voluntary, not compulsory
