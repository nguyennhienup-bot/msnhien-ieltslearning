🎯 Practice Exercises

Complete real IELTS-style essay topics

1

Exercise 1: Technology and Communication

📝 Topic:

"Some people believe that technology has made communication easier but has made relationships more superficial. Agree or disagree?"

Hint:
  • Consider both positive and negative aspects of technology on relationships
  • You can use a balanced approach: acknowledge one side but favor the other
  • Examples: social media, video calls, instant messaging, long-distance relationships
  • Target: 250-300 words, 4-5 paragraphs
2

Exercise 2: Higher Education

📝 Topic:

"University education is essential for a successful career. Do you agree or disagree?"

Hint:
  • Consider alternative paths like vocational training, apprenticeships
  • Discuss different industries (tech, business, trades)
  • You might present both sides but take a clear position
  • Examples: self-taught programmers, business entrepreneurs, licensed tradespeople
  • Target: 250-300 words, 4-5 paragraphs
3

Exercise 3: Environmental Protection

📝 Topic:

"Environmental protection should be the government's top priority. Do you agree or disagree?"

Hint:
  • Consider other government priorities: economy, healthcare, education
  • Discuss the balance between development and conservation
  • Think about long-term vs short-term consequences
  • Examples: climate change, renewable energy, pollution, wildlife protection
  • Target: 250-300 words, 4-5 paragraphs
4

Exercise 4: Social Media Impact

📝 Topic:

"Social media has had more negative than positive effects on society. Do you agree or disagree?"

Hint:
  • Acknowledge positive aspects: connection, community building, business opportunities
  • Discuss negatives: misinformation, mental health issues, cyberbullying, addiction
  • Consider different perspectives: youth vs adults, developed vs developing countries
  • You might argue for a balanced view or lean strongly one way
  • Target: 250-300 words, 4-5 paragraphs
